# DUBLIN/01 — PRICING MARKET RESEARCH

**Date of the study:** 6 October 2026 (prices relayed from the project's earlier research are dated 4–5 October 2026).
**Market:** Ireland (Dublin), EUR, VAT-inclusive consumer prices.
**Scope:** every product in `src/data/products.json` — 33 products (17 brand products, 16 DUBLIN/01 label products).
**Status:** proposal only. `products.json` and the frontend have **not** been modified. Waiting for "PRICING APPROVED".

## Method

1. Official brand site for Ireland, then official EU site.
2. Recognised retailers operating in Ireland (JD Sports IE, Footshop IE, Footpatrol IE, Zalando IE).
3. Large European retailers delivering to Ireland (Sneakersnstuff EU, i-Run, Care of Carl, Asphaltgold, Snowleader).
4. Regular price (RRP) and sale price are kept apart: a discounted listing never sets the recommended price.
5. No marketplace, second-hand or auction listing is used as a price source.

### How each price was read

- **Read directly on 6 October 2026:** nike.com/ie, footshop.ie, jdsports.ie, sneakersnstuff.com (EU).
- **Search result:** the price comes from a search-engine result for the page, not from the page itself. Weaker evidence.
- **Relayed:** adidas.ie, asics.com/ie, newbalance.ie and salomon.com refuse automated access from this machine (HTTP 403). Their prices are those recorded by the project's research on 4–5 October 2026 and should be re-checked by hand before approval.

Retailer search pages are cited as sources: results change over time.

## Confidence levels

- **HIGH** — several reliable sources agree.
- **MEDIUM** — correct but limited data, or the exact variant is not confirmed.
- **LOW** — hard to identify precisely, or one source only.
- **NEEDS REVIEW** — no recommendation: a price would be a guess.
