Two different things

Your legal right to cancel lasts 14 days and comes from Irish and EU consumer law. Our returns policy lasts 30 days and is something we choose to offer on top. One never replaces the other.

The 14-day right to cancel

  • You can cancel an online order for any reason within 14 days of the day you, or someone you name, receive the goods.
  • To cancel, tell us clearly in writing, for example through the contact page, before the 14 days end. You do not have to give a reason.
  • You then have 14 days to send the goods back. Unless we have told you we will cover it, you pay the direct cost of returning them.
  • We refund what you paid for the goods, including the standard delivery charge, within 14 days of being told you are cancelling. We may wait until we have the goods back, or proof that you sent them.
  • You may handle goods as you would in a shop. If they come back worn or damaged beyond that, the refund can be reduced.

The right to cancel does not apply to a few kinds of goods, such as items made to your specification, or sealed goods that cannot be returned for hygiene reasons once unsealed.

If something is faulty

Goods must be as described, fit for their purpose and of satisfactory quality and durability. If they are not:

  • within 30 days of delivery you can reject them and get a full refund;
  • otherwise you can choose a repair or a replacement, free of charge and within a reasonable time;
  • if that is not possible or does not solve the problem, you are entitled to a price reduction or a refund.

A fault that appears within 12 months of delivery is presumed to have been there from the start. Your contract is with us, the seller: you do not have to go to the manufacturer.
