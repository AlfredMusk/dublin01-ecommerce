Legal guarantee

Everything we sell is covered by the legal guarantee of conformity: if an item is faulty or not as described, you are entitled to a repair, a replacement, a price reduction or a refund from us, at no cost to you. EU law sets this guarantee at a minimum of two years; in Ireland you can bring a claim for up to six years from delivery.
