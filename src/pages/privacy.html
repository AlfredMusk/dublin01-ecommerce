What this site stores today

Your bag, your wishlist, the products you viewed recently and your cookie choice are kept in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Forms on this site (checkout, account, newsletter, contact) are not connected to any service yet: what you type in them is not sent or stored.
