The standard we follow

We build this site to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What that means in practice

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator.
  • Menus, the search, the bag and size pickers are announced to screen readers and return focus where you left off.
  • Text keeps sufficient contrast against its background, including text over photographs.
  • The page reflows down to a 320-pixel-wide screen and can be zoomed.
  • Moving content respects the “reduce motion” setting of your device. The home page slideshow pauses when you hover or focus it, and has a Pause button reachable by keyboard.
  • Forms have visible labels and explain errors in text.

Known limits

Product photographs come with a short text description, not a detailed one. We test with current versions of Chrome, Safari and VoiceOver; other combinations may behave differently.
