The DUBLIN/01 label

DUBLIN/01 is the shop and it is also our own label: a fictional line of clothing and accessories shown next to well-known sneaker brands. Those brands are referenced for demonstration only: there is no affiliation or partnership.

What we stock

Runners and terrace classics from Nike, adidas, New Balance, ASICS and Salomon, chosen for how they wear on wet streets as much as how they look. Alongside them, our own label: heavy cotton basics, technical outerwear and a few accessories.
