Why “/01”

Every season is an edition. The number resets when the shop changes enough to deserve it. This is the first one.

The DUBLIN/01 label

DUBLIN/01 is the shop and it is also our own label: clothing and accessories designed here and sold only here, next to the brands we stock.

What we stock

Runners and terrace classics from Nike, adidas, New Balance, ASICS and Salomon, chosen for how they wear on wet streets as much as how they look. Alongside them, our own label: heavy cotton basics, technical outerwear and a few accessories.

How we work

Prices include VAT. Standard delivery is free across Ireland on orders over €100, and you have 30 days to send something back. If it doesn’t fit, we would rather sort it out than lose you.
